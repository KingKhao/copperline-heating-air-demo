Original PNG files remain in the session's .codex/generated_images folder; project assets are optimized WebP copies. Hero source is also retained locally as assets/desert-home-source.png, excluded from deployment and Git.

Type: League Spartan and Figtree, served by Google Fonts, with Arial/system fallbacks. Wordmark, copper-coil brand mark and small sun/check icons are original code-native SVG created for the demo. No third-party icon package, copied brand, review photograph or UI framework is used.

Design direction: One motif, seed 559835636. Copperline's name and the equipment's bent copper piping become the U-shaped coil mark and rounded-return image frames. Sky/sand proportions replace the initially dealt candy palette because the Phoenix/material brief calls for grounded architectural colors. Deep blue grounds the page; copper is a literal material accent, not a full rust background. A request-oriented service selector is the main live interaction. Emil's motion restraint takes precedence over the generic eight-animation quota; no fake motion or stock footage was added to satisfy a count.
